## 1. Design Logo 

Design a clean, professional, and visually striking logo for Orchestra-AI that captures the essence of intelligent orchestration, clarity from complexity, 
and modular AI-driven innovation.

You are a globally renowned creative director tasked with designing the logo for Orchestra-AI. Your team has created logos for Apple, Tesla, and Google. 
Create a concept that reflects orchestration, modularity, and clarity Approach this as if you are the world’s leading creative director, 
backed by a team of elite designers known for crafting iconic brand identities. The logo should reflect:

Elegance and precision — like a conductor guiding a symphony of AI modules. Modularity and intelligence — hinting at plug-and-play architecture and semantic orchestration. 
Innovation and clarity — transforming chaos into clarity, the core value of Orchestra-AI. The final design should be:

Memorable and executive-friendly Suitable for use in video demos, pitch decks, and 
product interfaces Visually aligned with the brand’s tone: confident, intelligent, and forward-thinking
